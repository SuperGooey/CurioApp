Privacy Policy for Curio

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Curio is a media browser for files you already have on your own devices. Its privacy story is short, because Curio does not collect your data.

No data collection

Curio does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Your files stay on your device

Curio only accesses the folders you explicitly choose through the system file picker. Their contents are read locally to display and play them, and are never uploaded or sent anywhere.

No network activity

Curio does not transmit your files or any usage information to us or to any third party. Because Curio collects no data, there is nothing for us to sell, share, or delete.
